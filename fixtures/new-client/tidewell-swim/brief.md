# Tidewell Swim: new-client brief

Fictional prospect for demonstration. Supplied by the agency as explicit context for the intelligence canvas.

Tidewell Swim is a new swimwear and resort clothing brand launching in spring 2027. The founder designs in small batches and ships direct to customers.

## Objective
Test which creative approaches are worth scaling before committing a larger budget.

## Budget and channels
- Test budget: 8,000 USD over six weeks.
- Channels: Pinterest and Meta.

## Audience
Women 25 to 40 who plan coastal trips and shop online. Visually led; pins boards before buying.

## Brand tone
Relaxed, sunny, personal. The founder wants customers to understand why each piece is made the way it is.

## Constraints
- No discount language at launch.
- Images should look like real coastal places, not studio backdrops.
- The agency must say clearly what is a hypothesis; the founder does not want promises of results.
