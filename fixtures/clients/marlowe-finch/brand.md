# Marlowe & Finch: brand context

Fictional brand for demonstration. Synthetic data.

## Brand voice
Warm, thoughtful, a little old-fashioned. Writes like a letter from a small workshop. Uses first-person plural ("we") for the makers. Avoids exclamation marks and sales language.

## Audience
Adults 35 to 60 who value durable, well-made basics and are willing to pay more for shirts and trousers that last. Mostly buys two to three pieces per order. Reads captions and long product descriptions.

## Goals
1. Increase repeat purchases.
2. Lower cost per conversion on Pinterest and Meta.
3. Keep the brand from looking like a discount retailer.

## Constraints
- Never use discount or urgency language ("sale", "last chance", "hurry") in new creative.
- Do not make claims about ethical sourcing beyond what is on the About page.
- Always credit the person in a story if they gave permission; otherwise describe them generally.

## Standing instructions
- Reports open with conversions and cost per conversion.
- Describe creative by what is in it; never guess at the emotional reaction of customers.
