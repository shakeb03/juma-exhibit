# Ridgeline Outfitters: client brief

Fictional brand for demonstration. Synthetic data. Agency-private.

Ridgeline Outfitters sells technical outerwear and base layers direct to consumers. Annual paid media budget for this year is about 1.1 million USD, split mainly between Pinterest, Meta, and Google Search.

## This quarter
- Autumn is the strongest sales window. The team wants to lean into on-location imagery because internal surveys show customers trust photos taken in real conditions.
- Pinterest is treated as a discovery channel; Google Search captures demand that already exists.
- The client's marketing lead, Dana, reviews reports every Monday and prefers one page with a short list of next steps.

## Sensitivities
- A product recall notice on one jacket model in July is not to be mentioned in any public-facing material.
- Internal target cost per conversion is 30 USD or less.
