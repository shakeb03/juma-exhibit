# Ridgeline Outfitters: brand context

Fictional brand for demonstration. Synthetic data.

## Brand voice
Confident, plain-spoken, outdoorsy without being macho. Short sentences. Prefers concrete details (fabric weight, trail names) over adjectives. Never sarcastic.

## Audience
Adults 28 to 45 who hike, camp, or trail run at least monthly and buy technical layers once or twice a year. Research-minded; reads product specs before buying. Roughly even split between women and men.

## Goals
1. Grow online revenue from technical jacket and base-layer lines.
2. Keep return on ad spend above 4.0 across paid channels.
3. Build recognition of the Ridgeline name before the winter season.

## Constraints
- Do not claim waterproof ratings beyond those on the product page.
- No environmental or sustainability claims unless the agency has seen certification documents.
- Models and photography must show real outdoor conditions; no studio-faked weather.

## Standing instructions
- Weekly reports lead with revenue and ROAS, then conversions, then reach.
- Flag any week where spend changed by more than 20 percent from the prior week.
- Keep recommendations to three or fewer.
