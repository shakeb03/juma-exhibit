# Marlowe & Finch: client brief

Fictional brand for demonstration. Synthetic data. Agency-private.

Marlowe & Finch is a family-owned shirtmaker with a small mill and a workshop. Paid media budget is about 320,000 USD a year across Pinterest, Meta, and Google Search.
